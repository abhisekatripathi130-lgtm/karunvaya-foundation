04 — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Good questions deserve clear answers.

Can I donate right now?

No. Karunvaya Foundation is preparing verified donation infrastructure. The amount selector above is a demonstration only and does not create a pledge or payment.

Does selecting an amount save my details?

No. Selections are displayed only within your current browser page; no personal data or payment information is requested or submitted.

How will contributions be used?

We aim to support education and social welfare initiatives. Specific budgets, fundraising objectives and programme reports will be published once they are established and verified.

Will donors receive receipts or tax benefits?

Any future receipt and applicable tax-benefit information will depend on verified organisational approvals and processes. We do not currently claim 80G or other tax-deduction eligibility.

Can I transfer money to a volunteer's personal UPI account?

No. Please do not send money to individuals claiming to represent this initiative. Refer only to official, verified payment instructions after they are published.
