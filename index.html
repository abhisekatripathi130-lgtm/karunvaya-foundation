Karunvaya Foundation · Our belief

Karunvaya Foundation is being shaped around these two inseparable ideas. Our first chapter focuses on practical learning support for children and thoughtful, needs-led social welfare for communities. We intend to listen first, act responsibly, and measure what truly matters.

Privacy & transparency

Trust begins with clarity.

This static pre-launch website has no submission forms, cookies, analytics or payment processing. If we introduce those features later, we will publish suitable policies and data-handling details. Our organization name is a proposed identity; registration status must be confirmed before launch.

Your questions, answered

Clarity builds trust.

Is Karunvaya Foundation already registered?

This is a pre-launch website for a proposed initiative. We will publish verified registration and legal details after incorporation and verification.

Can I donate through this website?

Not yet. No payment details or donation gateway are provided while the necessary legal and banking arrangements are being completed.

How can I join as a volunteer?

Visit our volunteer page to learn about planned opportunities. Formal applications will open after safety, communication, and programme processes are in place.

Where will the foundation begin its activities?

We intend to begin with a local community pilot in India. Locations and schedules will be confirmed publicly before activities begin.
