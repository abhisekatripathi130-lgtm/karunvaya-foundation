Contact-form privacy

When you submit the secure contact form, Karunvaya Foundation stores your name, email address, enquiry category, message, the time of submission, and your recorded consent in its access-controlled website database. Only authenticated foundation administrators can read and manage these enquiries. Messages are not published and the form does not automatically send email.

The information is used to review and respond to enquiries and is kept until it is no longer needed for that purpose, subject to applicable obligations. Please avoid submitting sensitive identity documents, PAN numbers, payment credentials, medical information or unrelated personal data through this general contact form. For questions about your personal data, use the website's contact channels.
