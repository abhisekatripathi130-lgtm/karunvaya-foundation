Karunvaya / Social Welfare

Compassion that creates change.

We want welfare support to begin with listening and to preserve the dignity, agency and privacy of every individual.

Programme focus 02

Compassion made practical.

Karunvaya Foundation plans to work alongside communities to understand essential needs and connect vulnerable families with appropriate support.

Each activity will be guided by clear needs assessments, fair distribution practices and accountable record-keeping.

Planning stage: Drives, beneficiary selection and activity locations will be announced only when verified.
Proposed initiatives

Support that meets real needs.

01 / ESSENTIALS

Basic supplies

Needs-led support such as food essentials, clothing and seasonal necessities.

02 / GUIDANCE

Welfare information

Helping community members find credible information about accessible local services.

03 / CONNECTION

Community care

Working with trusted partners to respond respectfully to identified needs.

How we intend to act

People before publicity.

01

Listen and verify

Understand needs in a way that does not make support conditional on public exposure.

02

Distribute fairly

Use written criteria, responsible procurement and basic financial records.

03

Protect dignity

Respect consent, keep sensitive details private and avoid exploitative photographs or stories.
