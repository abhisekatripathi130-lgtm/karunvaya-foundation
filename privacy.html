Karunvaya / Privacy & Transparency

Trust is the heart of everything we do.

We want transparency about what our website does today, and what will only be introduced when appropriate safeguards are ready.

Pre-launch site

Information handled with care.

This static informational site has no online forms, payment processing, accounts or built-in analytics trackers. Routine hosting infrastructure may process technical connection logs.

We do not ask you to submit names, children’s details or payment information here. Before introducing data collection, the foundation intends to prepare a suitable privacy notice, consent process, retention arrangements and secure form handling.

This is a preliminary website notice, not a statement of completed legal registration or a substitute for a reviewed organisational privacy policy.
